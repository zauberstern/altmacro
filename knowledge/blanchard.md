# Blanchard, Amighini, Giavazzi

Series: `ltgdr`, `bund_ltgdr`, `oat_bund_bps`, `cabgdp1`.

The debt-ratio identity is the same accounting relation as Romer chapter 13. Uncovered interest parity is an arbitrage condition, not a player choice. A euro member has no nominal devaluation. France opens on that rule.

The OAT-Bund spread is the French 10-year minus the German 10-year, in basis points. It is a measurement, not a credit-default price.

Source: Macroeconomics, A European Perspective, chapters 18 to 23.
