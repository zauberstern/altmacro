# Romer, fiscal identity

Series: `ltgdr` as the interest rate when a country has no separate bill rate. `gdpn1` growth is `g` only inside a year that is not a currency splice.

The debt ratio moves with `(r - g)` times the ratio plus the primary balance over income. A constant headline deficit ratio does not explode. This card does not turn on a primary-balance control until a published debt ratio is in the catalog and the identity rebuilds it.

Source: Romer, Advanced Macroeconomics, chapter 13.
