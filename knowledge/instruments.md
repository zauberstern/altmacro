# Instruments

Series: `ltgdr` for the 10-year par-bond scenario. `mportret` is a listed equity total return, not a weight.

Modified duration of an annual par bond is `(1/y) * (1 - (1+y)^(-n))`. A 10-year bond at 4 percent is about 8.11. The scenario holds the decision-month yield. It is not a CRSP Treasury total return. Those returns were not readable.

SPX listed puts stay on the United States only. FX forwards, barriers, and the other chapter 1 and 2 products are cards. They stay unpriced until the named series is in the file. CDS and solved portfolio weights stay off.

Source: Chan, Guo, Lee, and Li, chapters 3.5 and 10.2. Wystup, chapter 2, is the deny list. Ahlawat chapter 6 is a regression, not holdings.
