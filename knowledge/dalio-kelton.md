# Dalio and Kelton

Series: `ltgdr`, `cpir`, `unemp1`, `cabgdp1`.

Both books accept the stocks and the sectoral identity. They disagree on what a wide spread means. The default chart shows the yields and the spread. It does not colour the line as a default or as proof that a sovereign cannot be forced. France in the euro is a currency user in Kelton's sense. The household financing constraint can be discussed. It is not printed as a fact on the line.

Source: How Countries Go Broke, chapters 1 to 3. The Deficit Myth, chapters 1 to 5.
